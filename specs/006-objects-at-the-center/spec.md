# Spec 006 — Contracts in the center's index

Kete-core spec 142, Kete Enterprise spec 100; doctrine D-062, R1 and R15.

## Requirements

- **FR-001 — Declared.** The manifest declares `contract` — unsigned, running; ended closes it —
  on the project, third party and person axes.
- **FR-002 — Said.** With its holdings, the app says each contract not ended, and those that ended
  in the last month: title, state, project, third party, who wrote it, where it opens.

## Proof

`tests/commitments.test.ts`: the kind passes its contract; an unsigned contract is said with who wrote
it; every object in the shape of `object.v1` with a declared state.
