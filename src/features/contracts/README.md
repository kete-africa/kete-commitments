# Contracts

Contracts, obligations, retentions and bonds, a funder's rules of spending, and `spending_check`
(spec 001). `domain/rules.ts` holds the whole logic of the check, pure: every agreement funding
the project must allow the expense; posts and words compare without case nor accents; a refusal
carries its agreement, its passage and what was found.

## The living model (spec 003)

A contract written with its signature day is a `contract.sign` the organization's policies decide
at the center (kete-core spec 114): refused with their sentences, stopped when they ask an approval,
written when they allow it or say nothing. What we owe, what the other party owes and what is to
recover are said as holdings of who wrote the contract (`contractHoldings`).
